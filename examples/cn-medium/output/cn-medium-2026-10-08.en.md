## How the metrics were produced

| Metric | Basis | Method | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| beta_stability | measured | Consistency of beta between the two halves of the same 180-return window. | 180d |
| beta_strength | measured | Positive OLS beta against the theme peer basket, scaled so beta=2 maps to 100; negative beta maps to zero. | 180d |
| factor_r2 | measured | Intercept OLS against the same-theme leave-one-out equal-weight peer basket using the latest 180 common daily returns; R² is scaled to 0–100, with at least 30 overlapping returns. | 180d |
| independence | measured | Derived by the CLI as 100 minus factor_r2; describes theme-price residuals only. | 180d |
| liquidity | measured | Percentile of mean raw close times share volume over the latest 30 valid sessions within 2,613 CNY stock/reference-ETF histories; ties use average ranks. Ticker-specific 1x/100x volume conversions were verified. This is not an exhaustive market population. | 30d |
