# 0.7: Compact presentation and Heavy-shaped Max

Heavy's reviewed members define market structure. Max (formerly Extreme) preserves them
and adds at least 40% measured Beta. Sparse groups get no inverse-count expansion bonus.
Existing business, measurement, evidence, sector and satellite gates remain.

## Presentation

Optional `coverage_plan.display_groups` contains four profile-keyed lists. Each row has
`id`, `name`, `themes`, and `reason`. The id is a constituent theme code, the name an ASCII
section label, and themes are disjoint known source themes. The reason explains economic
similarity. Combine adjacent sparse groups for readability; preserve original member themes,
branches, reasons, sources and observation duties. Do not merge unrelated duties just for size.

Light/Medium may use broader presentation. Heavy/Max must have identical group maps.
Membership nesting is independent of presentation. MD, TXT and token checks share the map.
References stay distinct instruments and never count toward entity proportions.

## Allocation

For Heavy count H and minimum r, research A = ceil(H*r) additional Beta. A Heavy group of h
members can add ceil(h*A/H). Choose groups by h/(2*new_g+1), then sourced market cap inside
the group. A 40/10 distribution expands by 16/4 at 40%. Rounding is the only group surplus.
Candidate shortages cannot transfer many seats into another group.

Stop at the minimum qualified expansion; plan/spec budgets remain ceilings. Validation
recomputes group ceilings from actual added count. Retained facts equal the validated Heavy
seed. Max cannot introduce a group absent from Heavy. Sector/satellite/export caps still bind.

Shortfalls retain a `needs_research` checkpoint and group availability diagnostics. Research
missing groups and resume; never weaken Beta gates or call an underfilled Max formal.
Archive Extreme outputs unchanged. Explicitly migrate input profile/budget/cap keys to `max`
and rebuild all tiers for this policy.

## Proof boundary

Test unequal group sizes, concentrated candidate supply, rounding, member preservation,
group maps, MD/TXT parity, references and stored validation. Rebuilding dated snapshots
does not refresh prices or independently establish issuer quality.

## Beta research budget

Beta uses broad business/token identity, complementarity to named core leaders and dated sourced market cap. Detailed profitability or protocol tokenomics is optional, never an admission dependency. Rank by equity/native-currency or circulating USD cap inside the planned distribution; keep listing, liquidity, measured Beta and evidence gates. This reduces research cost without describing market cap as proof of business durability. Missing caps remain research gaps.
