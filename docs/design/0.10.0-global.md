# 0.10.0 — Audited Global compositions

Date: 2026-10-18. Extends the three-round Global study with three implementation reviews.
This is a composition mode; single-market rules, profiles and the approved personal r6 inputs
remain unchanged. A local pilot is not a worldwide research run.

## Round 4: freeze the acceptance boundary

Require a separate coverage contract, passed explicitly to both composition and validation.
It declares market/theme groups, child duties, allowed instrument kinds, leader minima, named
inclusions/exclusions and ETF screening floors. Its full digest is pinned in the composition.
Validation against the original contract rejects an edited or self-shrinking denominator.

Source universes must independently qualify under the current policy. Reference-only gauges
can use a separately validated catalog; they cannot conceal companies/tokens as references.
Global leaders remain researched leader/peer entities from those source universes. Selection
does not rewrite a source or pretend its date is today's date.

## Round 5: evidence and identity review

Bind each selected ticker to one source and one sourced global identity. Legal identifiers and
economic entity IDs detect duplicate share classes/home/ADR lines; ticker spelling is never an
alias rule. Retain one primary line and research alternate lines outside the published selection.
Issuer mapping is a researched assertion, not machine proof of beneficial ownership.

ETF research needs dated assets, gross fees, spread, inception, full issuer-normalized holdings,
mandate/exposure identity and thirty dated USD notional observations. Recompute turnover and
holdings overlap; never convert average shares times today's price into a measured history.
Unknown, thin, expensive or stale funds block composition rather than gain a geography waiver.
High overlap or an identical mandate requires an evidenced coexistence review with distinct
duties; shared constituents with a company are reported without automatic deletion.

## Round 6: delivery and test review

Preserve single-market CLI build/validate. Add `compose-global`, `validate-global` and
`check-watchlist`; use a small offline module, no service or database. A portable output includes
exact source JSON copies, the pinned contract, authoritative composition, validation, three TXT
views and English Markdown/HTML. Independent validation reopens all sources and checks exports.
Publish atomically only after all research contracts and three TXT round trips pass.

The [official TradingView import instructions](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000487233-how-to-import-or-export-a-watchlist/)
require TXT, exchange prefixes and comma-separated symbols. The main Global TXT is ticker-only.
Two optional grouped views use the repository's existing `###` convention, count headings toward
the 1,000-token contract and retain identical ticker sets. Syntax checks do not establish account
limits, feed subscriptions, exact symbol availability or successful UI import.

Tests must exercise duplicate issuers, source mutation, future/stale data, missing/shrunken duties,
fund floors, observed turnover/overlap, invalid delimiters, empty groups, duplicate symbols,
capacity, output collisions, relocation and post-publication tampering. Then run the full existing
suite, Ruff, skill validation and a reproducible dated pilot. Keep synthetic test success distinct
from the pilot's limited market research and from a full Global production universe.

See [the maintained Global contract](../../references/global.md) for the final interface.
