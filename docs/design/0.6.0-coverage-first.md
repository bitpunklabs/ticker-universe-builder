## Diagnosis

Legacy Core reviews market structure and leaders industry by industry before extension. Additions
explain missing observation; removals explain surviving coverage. Agents judge, deterministic
pool operations/validation/rendering apply changes. Extension ranking is separate from Core.

The new skill retained snapshots, measurements, evidence, hashes and recovery, but weighted role
buckets filled capacity before protecting the necessary roster. One representative per display
theme and cross-bucket fallback let topic splits gain floors/weights. Heavy had only 50% core,
Extreme 35%, without hard Beta caps. ANCHOR labels and count completion did not establish leadership.
Crypto Heavy 260 was disproportionate to Core 53.

## Final design after review

Parameter changes alone could not repair missing branch duties or representative ordering.

- Separate stable economic sectors/branches from display themes; absolute sector caps govern extensions.
- Declare every necessary leader/peer with business, continuing quality and T1/T2 evidence.
  Legacy roles/Core retention alone cannot certify admission.
- Light/Medium admit leaders only; Medium covers >=70% of the reviewed roster. Heavy protects all
  necessary leaders/peers before optional satellites, capped at 20%; Extreme at 35% overall.
- Require matching qualified Heavy before Extreme, with retained facts and satellite-only additions.
  Budgets are ceilings; in 0.6 quality caps took precedence over the old 45% fill target.
- Freeze Core text/SHA-256; resolve retain/replace/remove/pending for every original instrument.
  Pending blocks Heavy/Extreme; selected replacements need sourced identity/unit/duty explanations.
- Keep indices/yields/FX/commodity references distinct from entities; retain exact type/proxy limits
  and export them in the same 1,000-token TXT without consuming entity seats.
- Crypto declares ecosystem_id/token_role and concrete duties; two related tokens are not automatically
  two networks. Structural use/value capture/supply/liquidity principles survive without applying
  one historical strict new-token revenue rule to all established leaders.

## Implementation

Add coverage_core.py; reuse contracts, measurement, rendering/hashes and checkpoints. No database,
optimizer or network runtime. Default selection: validate plan/admissions, audit migration, select
necessary representatives, then rank qualified satellites within economic capacity, revalidate/render.
0.6 extension ranking used sector weight/holdings and measurement scores; 0.7 replaces it with
Heavy-shaped Max and sourced cap ranking. Legacy 0.4/0.5 requires explicit archived policy.
Plan/Core/reference changes require a rebuilt Heavy before extension; maintenance shares final gates.

CN review prioritizes banking/insurance/power/consumer/agriculture branches and separates fibre from
grid equipment. US preserves US-listed foreign scope and differentiated financial/software/drug/
logistics/utility/consumer functions. Crypto preserves multi-venue scope and exact contract units;
BTC/ETH/SOL are entities, tokenized gold is not RWA protocol exposure, VET/VTHO are not two networks.
