## Review: tools migrated, selection order lost

Legacy `_core.md` requires an addition to explain the missing observation and a removal to
explain whether its industry remains observable. It explicitly rejects weakening structural
coverage because AI is popular or consumption is weak. `phase-survey.md` establishes market
structure; `phase-category.md` reviews industry stages and leaders before adding members.
`pool_agent.py` gathers judgements in stages, repairs locally and then consolidates;
`pool_ops/pool_validation/pool_render` apply operations deterministically. Legacy `pool_ext.py`
is separate from Core, ranking structural extensions by relatively stable size. Liquidity and
quantitative extensions have distinct duties. Its advantage is the curated existing pool and
industry-by-industry review, not an inherently smarter, more complex runtime.

The new architecture correctly retains standard-library code, snapshots, measurements,
evidence, hashes, resumable builds and deterministic rendering. However, it turns long-term
maintenance into filling capacity from a large candidate bench. `_select_stage` takes one
representative per theme, then expands through weighted role buckets; a short bucket can be
filled from another. Heavy has only 50% core and Extreme 35% core, without hard extension caps.
An `ANCHOR` label does not prove leadership. The historical constructor labels the first two
members of each group as anchors, forcing the remaining Core members to compete with ordinary
candidates. `build_run` declares completion from counts. Splitting themes increases floors and
weights, giving popular areas two budget advantages. Crypto's default Heavy of 260 cannot
meaningfully correspond to a Core of 53.

Audit findings: CN Heavy retains 313/463 original securities, US 298/378 and Crypto 47/53
original codes. CN sectors 10+11 rise from 20.95% to 32.44% of entities; banks fall from 8 to 2
and alcoholic beverages from 9 to 2. US adds financial names but loses WFC/C/USB; larger software
and pharmaceutical groups defer important existing representatives to Extreme. Of Crypto's
115 additions from 260 to 375, risk appetite/gaming/social account for 61. These are output
structure facts, not independently verified leader omission rates or statistical Beta shares.

## Initial proposal and revisions after review

Initial proposal: raise the core share, lower theme floors, restore banking/consumption weights
and enlarge the candidate bench.

Review rejected parameter changes alone. Two banks per group still cannot cover business
branches; more candidates do not repair representative ordering; high Core overlap can also
copy inherited classification errors. The final design is:

| Problem | Final constraint |
|---|---|
| Popular theme splits capture budget | Economic sectors/branches are independent of display themes. Absolute parent-sector caps and stable weights govern optional extensions; more headings do not create budget. |
| Any ticker counts as coverage | Each important branch declares a minimum depth and necessary representatives. Heavy covers all important branches and reviewed necessary representatives. |
| ANCHOR labels stand in for leadership | Separate admission records identify leader / peer / satellite, with business representation, quality reasoning and tier 1 or 2 evidence. Legacy roles do not promote themselves. |
| Beta expands before the backbone is complete | An incomplete backbone blocks formal output and returns resumable diagnostics. Light/Medium admit no satellites; Heavy caps them at 20%, Extreme at 35%. These are adjustable engineering starting points, not industry laws. |
| Extreme repairs Heavy | Extreme requires a validated Heavy with the same date and plan. All necessary representatives belong in Heavy first; additions are qualified satellites only. |
| Padding to reach 45% growth | Budgets are ceilings and may remain unused. Quality takes precedence when the 35% overall satellite cap conflicts with the old 45% expansion target. |
| Missing Core members are unexplained | Freeze the original TXT and SHA-256. Record retain/replace/remove/pending for every code and verify retained/replacement entities enter Heavy. Pending decisions block Heavy/Extreme. |
| ETFs replace indices or yields | References are separate from entity budgets and measurement factors. Preserve instrument type, observation purpose, original code and evidence; export them alongside entities. |
| Crypto duplicates one ecosystem | Admissions declare ecosystem_id/token_role. Stable branches express concrete duties. Satellites declare distinct_from and incremental value; different tokens in one ecosystem do not automatically represent two networks. |

Light is a concise leader-only layer, not every leader in a market. Medium covers at least 70%
of the plan's reviewed leaders. Heavy/Extreme include all reviewed leaders and necessary peers.
Shares are checked against the same coverage plan, not an unknown market-wide leader population.
Every market follows the same workflow; researched inputs supply market differences without
market-specific selectors.

## Minimal implementation

Add one `coverage_core.py`, reusing candidate eligibility, measurements, evidence, hashes,
rendering and checkpoints. Introduce no network runtime, database or optimizer. The default
policy is coverage-first. Historical 0.4/0.5 inputs replay only with an explicitly archived
policy. Validation still checks old file integrity without certifying 0.6 coverage.

The snapshot adds `coverage_plan`: scope, four entity budgets, stable economic-sector weights
and caps, necessary branch representatives, references and Core migration records. Candidates
add `admission`: selection role, economic branch, minimum depth and business/quality evidence.
At this version, satellites also require measured Beta and an independent contribution to the
backbone. The agent judges businesses; code checks closure, consistency, budgets, coverage,
identity and provenance. A non-empty field does not prove a company is a leader.

Order: normalize, audit backbone and migration, include necessary representatives by depth,
validate Heavy, rank eligible Beta inside remaining parent-sector capacity, validate again,
then render. Extension ranking uses parent-sector weight/current holdings followed by existing
measurement scores among evidence-qualified candidates. Display-theme ordering, counts and
weights do not allocate economic budget. Qualified builds with unused capacity are `complete`;
an incomplete backbone is `needs_research`. Never publish a falsely qualified Extreme.

References do not masquerade as ordinary candidates to bypass eligibility. Their independent
contract checks strong evidence, dates, instrument type and observation purpose. Direct indices
and yields may have no volume; they still consume TradingView's 1,000-token export limit without
consuming entity seats. ETFs and indices, or spot and futures, are not automatically equivalent.

Maintenance keeps the current change operations and applies the same final coverage checks.
Changes to the plan or Core decisions require new research and a rebuilt Heavy before Extreme.
`MOVE`/`REMOVE` cannot bypass backbone requirements.

## Market research rules

CN: prioritize national/joint-stock/regional bank duties, insurance underwriting classes,
power-operation types, consumption subcategories and agriculture. Separate optical
communications from grid equipment.

US: use the US-listed scope, not US-domiciled companies only. Review banking, underwriting/
brokerage, software functions, drugs/devices, logistics, utilities and consumption formats.
HAS toys/IP do not count as household chemicals.

Crypto: preserve an explicit multi-venue scope. BTC/ETH/SOL belong to entities. XAUT's gold-price
exposure differs from RWA protocols; VET/VTHO do not represent two independent networks.
Check identities and eligibility such as GRAM/STX first. PEPE contract units and KHC prefixes
require explicit conversion rather than automatically restoring strings.

The legacy Crypto ADD five-gate policy is a version-specific strict new-token strategy. Do not
turn it into a universal requirement that every established network leader generate holder
revenue. Preserve its principles: order-book eligibility first, real use/value capture, supply
risk, residual information and stricter admission than retention. New satellites meet the
version's measurement gates and explain incremental information. Industry-specific facts
still require research; a global quality score does not replace it.

## Validation and delivery boundaries

Tests cover: bank backbone before high-scoring semiconductors; backbone invariance under theme
splits/reordering/news heat; failure on missing necessary representatives; no certification
from legacy anchor labels alone; no Light/Medium satellites; hard Heavy/Extreme caps; rejection
of missing, unqualified or differently planned Heavy seeds; explained Core removals and
instrument conversions; references preserved without consuming entity budgets; maintenance and
revalidation cannot bypass gates; repaired failures can resume.

Produce per-item migration audits in temp for the user's three Core pools and the previous six
outputs, preserving originals. These are research queues, not automatically certified new
production universes. Do not fabricate financial or leadership evidence for a thousand companies
from old TXT files. Until individual research is complete, strict generation should block
formal publication. Completed repairs establish contracts and passing regressions; they do not
establish that all old candidates are verified leaders, that TradingView imports work, or that
subsequent trading performance has been tested.
