# Global coverage research checklist

Use this when designing a new Global contract. It is a starting denominator for research, not
an admitted roster, market-wide census, national quota or promise of alpha. Record scope changes
before selection; leave a worldwide build unqualified when its mandatory researched duties lack
credible instruments. A bounded implementation pilot must say `scope_level: "pilot"`.

## Geography and index questions

| Research area | Duties and common errors to examine |
|---|---|
| Global/developed/emerging | Choose an all-country anchor and useful regional comparisons; audit actual countries and provider classification rather than equating World with all-country |
| US | Broad market, non-financial growth/innovation, Dow and smaller-company breadth when useful; avoid repeating near-identical wrappers |
| China A-shares | Broad domestic market and differentiated innovation/size styles; do not substitute offshore China for A-shares |
| Hong Kong/offshore China | Hong Kong broad regime and distinctive technology/internet exposure; distinguish H-shares, A-shares and US wrappers |
| Japan | Broad float-cap and price-weighted regimes can have distinct jobs; native-session versus USD-wrapper effects remain explicit |
| Taiwan and Korea | Country breadth plus essential foundry/memory/electronics roles; country concentration is not full semiconductor-chain coverage |
| India | Country breadth, with banking/IT only when differentiated; compare credible lower-fee country wrappers rather than defaulting to the familiar name |
| Pan-Europe and euro area | Different country sets; euro-area coverage cannot silently replace UK, Switzerland or Nordics |
| UK/Germany/France | Distinct native regimes and global operating franchises; inspect price versus return-index variants |
| Switzerland/Netherlands/Nordics | Important industrial, semiconductor, healthcare and brand franchises; do not force a country ETF when region coverage and exceptional issuers suffice |
| Canada/Australia | Country regimes and differentiated resources, finance and infrastructure; research dual corporate/listing structures individually |
| Brazil/Mexico | Native regimes and selected businesses; listing domicile and revenue markets can differ |
| Singapore/ASEAN | Singapore and Indonesia are useful separate research questions; review Malaysia/Thailand/Vietnam explicitly, refusing tiny/thin country funds |
| Middle East/Africa | Review Saudi and South African gauges and differentiated franchises; assess Israel/UAE/Turkey and other extensions without claiming all are covered |

Exact indices, variants, TradingView venues, live status and wrappers must be verified. A market
can have a direct index without a country ETF or a forced company seat. Do not assert a numerical
percentage of world coverage without a defined denominator and current measured data.

## Economic families and child duties

Preserve these distinctions in the coverage ledger even when navigation uses fewer parents.
Normally investigate 1–3 genuine leaders per operating duty; composite exceptions need reasons.
An ETF can supply breadth only where its holdings genuinely cover that function.

| Family | Child duties to examine |
|---|---|
| Compute/foundry | Processors, custom silicon and manufacturing have different value capture |
| Semiconductor equipment/materials | Lithography, deposition/etch, inspection and materials |
| Memory/storage | HBM/DRAM, NAND and storage/control mechanisms |
| EDA/IP | Design tools and licensable IP; often concentrated leadership |
| Servers/connectivity | Servers, networking, optical, PCB and interconnect are not interchangeable |
| Data centers/thermal | Asset operators, power and cooling; distinguish REIT and industrial economics |
| Cloud/platforms | Cloud operation, distribution platforms and advertising |
| Enterprise software/services | Enterprise applications, data infrastructure and IT services |
| Cybersecurity | Distinct security functions and credible basket breadth |
| Automation/robotics | Controls, motion, sensing, industrial versus surgical robotics |
| Grid/electrification | Equipment, cable, engineering and distribution |
| Power/nuclear | Regulated utilities, merchant generation, uranium fuel and nuclear equipment |
| Renewables/storage | Solar, wind, inverters and storage systems |
| Auto/batteries | Vehicles, cells and essential components; do not duplicate storage indiscriminately |
| Pharma/biotech | Established franchises, innovation and research breadth; obesity care is a child duty |
| Medtech/life sciences/care | Devices, diagnostics/tools and payer/provider businesses |
| Banks/insurance | Different financial operating models; no bank quota for every country |
| Payments/consumer finance | Payment networks, lenders and differentiated fintech |
| Exchanges/data/asset management | Distinct market infrastructure and capital-light franchises |
| Oil/gas/services | Production, LNG/midstream, services and refining as needed |
| Mining/metals | Copper, iron ore, gold and critical minerals; distinguish miners from commodity prices |
| Agriculture/inputs | Food inputs, fertilizers and equipment; no single business spans the whole chain |
| Aerospace/defense | Civil aircraft/engines and defense have different drivers |
| Machinery/materials | Equipment, engineering, process chemicals and materials |
| Transport/logistics | Shipping, logistics, airports and transport assets |
| Staples/brands | Food, beverage, household and distinctive local/global brands |
| Premium consumption/retail | Luxury, retail and travel; broad discretionary is not pure luxury |
| Digital consumption | Commerce, content, gaming and regional platform franchises |
| Telecom operators | Operator economics separate from equipment suppliers |
| Real assets/infrastructure | Property, towers and other infrastructure; do not repeat the data-center duty |

Small optional commodity, FX/yield and BTC/ETH anchors can explain context. Protocols remain
entities; equity ETF rules cannot be reused to mislabel physical-commodity or crypto ETPs.
Quantum/eVTOL/pure humanoids and other uncertain niches stay deferred if no credible business
or fund exists. Established automation or aerospace does not certify those narrower themes.

## Basket versus leader decisions

For concentrated franchises, investigate the leaders first; keep a basket only for an additional
important breadth duty. For dispersed industries, investigate a basket and essential distinct
leaders. Do not claim stock-basket outperformance without the prescribed one-/two-year and
largest-contributor-removal tests. Familiar names, size and recent strength alone do not prove
leadership, liquidity or global significance.

Compare same-job ETFs using exact mandates/holdings, robust trading/spread evidence, recognition,
scale, tracking and fees after the [Global admission floors](global.md) pass. Shared holdings
between a leader and a basket do not automatically make either redundant. Same-benchmark ETF
wrappers require a choice; other substantial overlap needs a documented distinct-duty review.
