# Germany equities overlay

Read [shared equity rules](equity-common.md) first. Starter themes describe listed duties;
current budgets come from the [coverage plan](../coverage-plan.md), not display weights.

## Identity

- `XETR` preferred; `FWB` accepted. Same-company lines share identity.
- One to six alphanumeric characters; leading digits are valid (`4GLD`, `1U1`).

## Economic structure

- Preserve autos, software, electrical equipment, chemicals and insurance/reinsurance.
- The starter omits oil-major/payments/data-center duties; research transition/AI observations
  through actual listed electrical-equipment and utility businesses.

## Adverse flags

| Code | What it is |
|---|---|
| `delisting_offer` | Subject to a delisting offer (Delisting-Angebot) |
| `prime_standard_breach` | In breach of a Prime Standard obligation |
| `squeeze_out` | In a squeeze-out of minority shareholders |

## Research fields

- DAX/MDAX/SDAX/TecDAX changes, squeeze-outs and delisting offers.

Report language: `de`, plus English when different. Use the closest [worked input](../../examples/README.md); no dedicated example ships.
