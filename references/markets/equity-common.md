# Shared equity rules

Read this before the requested market overlay. [Coverage](../coverage-plan.md),
[depths](../tier-profiles.md) and [measurement](../measurement.md) apply to every equity market.

## Scope and identity

Common shares and needed index/sector gauges are in scope. Exclude warrants, rights, shells,
preferred/duplicate lines and unconfirmed pre-revenue listings by default; use reviewed market
exceptions where appropriate. Confirm active listing, exact TradingView venue and usable turnover/
price variation. One economic entity gets one line; audit share-class/ADR/dual-listing duplicates.
Cash-management, money-market and ultra-short funds are excluded by instrument type.

## Classification and replacement

Map durable business models, pricing drivers and supply-chain duties from issuer disclosures.
Use one primary theme; secondary exposures belong in evidence/tags. Keep each market's distinct
structure and the plan's necessary representatives. Provider industries and recent returns cannot
replace economic duties. Deleting the last representative needs a qualified successor or explicit
sourced retirement. Preserve direct gauges separately from tradable proxies.

Economic weights/caps come from the reviewed plan; starter display weights do not allocate current
seats. Heavy/Max share merged display groups, and Max follows Heavy proportions. Cold sectors
remain covered; sparse related groups may merge without changing member facts.

## Funds and factors

Compare a sector fund with an equal-weight daily-rebalanced basket of its represented businesses
on full one- and two-year windows. Tracking plus positive excess on both makes it a redundancy
candidate, subject to preserved observation duty. Drop the largest contributor and repeat before
removal. Poor tracking can indicate a distinct factor; holdings overlap alone is insufficient.
See [measurement](../measurement.md) for numerical fit/robustness thresholds and missing horizons.

Regress equities against their own theme gauge, not the broad index. Without a suitable fund,
use a disclosed same-theme peer basket. Suggested fields: listing/quote status, local-currency
20/60-session turnover, theme-relative beta/downside beta/R²/excess/volatility/drawdown,
63/126/252-session stability and sector-appropriate quality/disclosures.

## Reports

Author local names, reasons, parent labels and measurement prose in the registered report language;
provide `report_translations.en` for English human content. English is always generated alongside
non-English companions; `--language` changes only the companion. Theme codes/names stay ASCII
for TradingView. [Output contract](../output-artifacts.md).
